Is it worth !

July 6, 2023 / 2 min read Read on Medium ↗

In the enigmatic dance of life, we confront the futile nature that often lingers within the realms of our existence. Reality, a deceptive mirage, whispers its oracular secrets, leaving us questioning the very fabric of our perceptions. Yet, amidst this paradoxical labyrinth, we find resilience and solace. We learn to navigate the ethereal intricacies, cherishing the transient moments and savoring the beauty of the journey. For it is in the ephemeral enchantment of our travels that we discover the true essence of our souls, defying the illusory confines of a world that may be but a captivating charade.

Below is a poem that I wrote way back when I was unknown to the intricate details about technicalities of a poem but It feels home whenever I read this piece….

Is it worth having a heart
So full of compassion
When there is none to give,
Is it worth having a wish
When you know
It can never be fulfilled.

Is it worth to cry
For something lost
When nothing gets changed by tear,
Is it worth to be proud
When you know
You are going to miss the task
you accomplished.

Then what is life all about
If not a nuance foretold
And a doomed algorithm called fate.

What are we worth
If reality is a hoax,
May be we are sum
Getting bigger and brighter,
While on journey
rather on reaching destination.
