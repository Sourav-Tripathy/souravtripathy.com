In a Subversion of Life

Between Logic and Living

June 28, 2025 / 1 min read Read on Substack ↗

A body in motion remains in motion ,
unless acted upon by grief.
But what of souls ?
They decelerate not from resistance,
but from remembering.

Let us consider:
Let x be time,
Let f(x) be love - bounded,
continuous, but not differentiable at heartbreak.

Entropy grows.
Every ordered morning leads to chaotic dusk.
Even hope bleeds into thermodynamic equilibrium.

We are quantum:
superposed between becoming and collapsing
measured only when observed,
and always changed by the looking.

Life isn't linear.
It's a Fourier transform of sorrow and joy
complex, with hidden frequencies
only evident in death's inverse.

So integrate it not over time,
but over meaning,
and you may find that limit does not exist
yet still converges.

Q.E.D. - Quite Elegantly Dying.
