Of Circuits and Citrus

March 30, 2025 / 4 min read Read on Substack ↗

I wake up sweating through my grey t-shirt. March’s heat may be high, though spring is yet to be over in the traditional sense of months. The fan whirs and the cooler exhales a damp static, probably all the water has been used, and the motor is dry. Why the sweat? I got curious, and the first thought that came to my mind was, maybe a bad dream…

I walk to the basin sink and start brushing my teeth, foam rising with each movement of my hand to clean my teeth while my mouth starts to feel the chemical mint of the toothpaste. I stare at the mirror above the basin, and all I could see was my face as if it were trying to solve a juxtaposition, maybe half man, half algorithm… Of what, I can’t seem to remember…

I pour water into the kettle and wait for it to scream while I put two spoonfuls of instant coffee into my blue ceramic mug. I pour the boiling water and see the blackness blooming upward, like a reverse galaxy collapsing with white trails. I glance to the side and see two oranges in the green polythene, left from last week's buy. I bring them out to see if they are fit to eat.

I return to my desk with both oranges in the bowl and the alchemist of urgency in the other hand. I sit down, and just as I take the first sip, I feel a bit burnt. The coffee was too hot. Maybe it was punishing me because it sensed that i am going to call it alchemist of urgency here….

I pick one orange, claw my nail into the rind of the orange while it sprays out a scent with tiny citrus drops as shrapnel. As I wave off the skin on one side, I see veins and roots through it, lit by the morning sunlight coming from the half opened window…

Coffee cools. I chew a slice - sour, then sweet, then neither. The bitterness in the mug sits adjacent, patient. It occurs to me this is not a Sunday, it’s a collision. The coffee is a command -wake, function, and linear, like a circuit… The orange is a question… why dissolve? Why not ferment? Together, they feel like an abstract language with no verbs. I count the orange seeds : one, two, three ,as I spit them into the bowl sequentially. They gleam like silicon chips. Maybe coffee stains, citrus ghosts were humming something.

I sip at last, lukewarm. The bitterness has softened. The orange’s tang lingers. I get hit with a pang of realization… Existence is not an addition, it’s a residue. The thing that sticks when logic evaporates. The heat, the dream I forgot, the fan’s whir, the number of seeds I spit out… they are not variables, they are stains… evidence of a math that exists only in the body's ledger.

Coffee and oranges now no longer feel like objects somehow. They are archetypes… coffee : the alchemist of urgency (grind, brew, and consume). Oranges : the anarchists of sweetness (peel, tear, revel). Together they are like a battery , positive and negative terminals sparking a pulse… the throbbing thing between survival and savoring. It’s not a rhythmic binary… it feels like something born out of a collision between circuits and citrus.

A vibration that hums: To exist is both a machine and monsoon. To short-circuit, to ferment, to burn and burst all at once. It speaks as if we all are proceeding to chaos while being wired to systems that crave order.

As I chew slices while sipping coffee in between, I realize the machine in me demands coffee’s clarity… the ruthless distillation of purpose, while the soul hungers for a mess just like the wobbling path made by white threads on the orange that are not yet colonized by logic. In those moments of introspection, I see it….silicon dreams of order, of clean lines and solved equations. Soul dreams in stains….in the tang that lingers after the swallow… One is the grid, the other is the vine that cracks it…..

Yet they need each other, just like coffee’s bitterness makes the orange sweeter. The orange’s chaos makes the coffee kinder. Together they feel like a dialectic… a synapse firing between the programmed and the possible……

I see for the first time while I hold both in my hands…

Silicon and soul grinding against each other, generating the pulse…. keeping the battery alive… as if they are trying to resist something… Maybe the silicon craves efficiency but fears rigidity… the soul wants mess but fears dissolution ..Maybe all they are trying is to not be reduced to just inputs and outputs.

The second orange remains. I leave it…

let it linger on…

let it accuse the silence…
