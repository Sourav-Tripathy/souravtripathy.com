We Are All Prisms in Sunlight

Notes on Beauty, Uselessness, and the Refusal to Move

April 27, 2025 / 3 min read Read on Substack ↗

I wake to summer sunlight carving itself through the cardboard-closed skylight, blade-sharp and insistent. I put on my specs, and there it is…VIBGYOR smeared across the ceiling like a child’s finger-paint.

Seventh-grade physics stirs somewhere inside me: Newton’s disc, the obedient pirouette of white from a spinning rainbow. Prisms and their clipped certainties.
I know what’s happening….refraction, angles, something crystalline intercepting the sun. My mind catalogs it, obedient as ever.
But knowledge sits brittle in my hands, a dry, crackling thing next to the wet immediacy pooling in the room.

I walk out, splash water on my face, sit at the laptop. Open it. Close it. Return to bed. Lie there for an hour, maybe two, while the colors pulse…
Breakfast happens by the mechanical act of chewing... but my skull is a cathedral echoing with that light.

Not curiosity, not obsession. Something softer…Like a bruise you keep pressing to feel its ache.

Murakami would’ve made this a portal. A jazz track would start playing from the ceiling, or a woman with a star-shaped birthmark would call to say, The rainbow is a bridge. Cross it.

Camus would scoff, light a cigarette, mutter that absurdity is the only truth worth bowing to…

But me?

I’m neither mystic nor philosopher. Just a body in pajamas, caught between the wanting to do and the doing of nothing.

I try to code. Fail. Pick up a book. Sentences swim.
The colors hum louder….like a choir of unanswerables :

Why does beauty haunt more than it comforts ?

Why does the universe bother folding itself into a rented bedroom ?

I’m not seventh-grade me anymore, scribbling lab reports with certainty that science could cage the sublime…Now I see , the sublime chews through bars. It lives in the cracks, the morning light, the way a rainbow dies without fanfare when the sun shifts.

By evening, the ceiling is blank.
The colors didn’t fade…they just left, like guests who knew not to overstay.
I don’t feel empty. Or full. Just… hyphenated.
A bridge between Newton’s equations and Camus’ void, between wanting meaning and refusing to hunt it.

There’s a rebellion in letting a moment be, untheorized, unoptimized.
It feels pragmatism is a language I’ve forgotten…

Maybe the light was never asking to be understood.
Maybe it just wanted a witness…a pair of unwashed eyes to hold it, hour after hour, in the quiet rebellion of it’s stillness.

The sun moves. I move…
Nothing is solved…
Everything is felt…
And isn’t that the most absurd yet necessary thing of all ?
