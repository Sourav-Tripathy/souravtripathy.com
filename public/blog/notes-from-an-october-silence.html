Notes from an October Silence

November 7, 2025 / 1 min read Read on Medium ↗

To live knowing life is absurd, and still to live as I wish…
That awareness strips me of emotion.

Yet some nights, when there’s no wish to read, write, or code,
I sit alone and open Instagram, then WhatsApp, again and again…
hoping someone will message,
And that a few words exchanged might make me feel seen.

No one does.
Even those I try to keep close drift quietly into the static.

It’s October …cruel as always.
November will follow, then December, colder still.
I’ll carry myself through them, as I have for all these years.

And somewhere, in some future night,
I’ll write these same words again,
forgetting that I’ve written them before.

Repetition will occur.
And perhaps, through that repetition,
I will survive…
