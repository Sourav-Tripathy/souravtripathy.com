Ecstasy of fallen flowers

July 1, 2023 / 1 min read Read on Medium ↗

In the delicate symphony of nature’s elements, raindrops descend from the heavens, gracing the earth with their ethereal presence. As they touch the ground, an ecstasy unfurls, akin to fallen flowers finding solace. Petals, longing for release, are gently caressed by each droplet, creating a profound connection. The first rain unveils a poignant tableau where the fragrant whispers of heaven’s grace intertwine, allowing fallen flowers to find eternal embrace and serenity within the embrace of the earth.

So Below is a poem that is about the ignition of an ecstasy

In rain’s embrace, fallen flowers delight,
Love’s whispers dance on a summer night.
Ecstasy blooms, subtle and serene,
A tender longing, in rain’s unseen.
The scent of petrichor, secrets unfold,
Two souls entwined, their story untold.
In murmurs of thunder, passion takes flight,
A dance in the rain, on this enchanted night.
With each raindrop’s touch,
love’s yearning ignites,
In twilight’s glow, where desire alights.
Memories linger, like petals in the rain,
In this transient moment,
ecstasy of fallen flowers arise.
