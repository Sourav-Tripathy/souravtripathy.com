The Calculus of Falling

A Descent Beyond Reason

March 12, 2025 / 4 min read Read on Substack ↗

I am falling….

Not the swift, gasping plunge of a skipped heartbeat — this is a drift, as if the universe has snipped its puppet strings. No ground yawning up, no sky recoiling.

Just… absence….

My arms float outward, crucified by inertia. Newton’s first law, I think faintly. An object in motion stays in motion… unless acted upon by a force. But what force exists here? The dark swallows sound, light, even time. My breath hitches — not from fear, but calculation.

How fast am I accelerating?

Where’s the drag of air resistance?

I should be terrified. And I am, the animal part of me, somewhere deep down, is on alert, firing warning signals like a malfunctioning Geiger counter.But another layer of me detaches, cool and observational. No terminal velocity. That’s the first clue. Terminal velocity requires atmosphere, molecules to collide with, friction to balance gravity. Here, there’s nothing. Just vacuum and… me.

I twist, trying to get a sense of up, down, something.

Useless…

There’s no direction to anchor to. No horizon to cling to. My stomach churns, but not from acceleration. There’s no gravity. That’s the second clue. If I’m falling, why isn’t there a mass pulling me? A black hole? But no. No tidal forces. No stretching, no tearing. My body feels… whole..

Panic and curiosity duel. I close my eyes — or do I ?

The darkness behind my lids matches the void outside.

Am I even real ?

I pinch my arm. Pain sparks, sharp and bright. Okay, tactile feedback. Not a simulation. Or if it is, a convincing one.

Time fractures. Minutes? Hours? I hum , testing for Doppler shifts. No echo. The sound dies inches from my lips. Sound needs a medium, I recite, like a student cramming for an exam. No air here. But then — how am I breathing? The thought cracks my clinical calm. My lungs inflate, deflate. Oxygen from where?

Think...I beg my synapses...

If this were space, I’d be dead. Frozen, suffocated, boiled. If this were a dream… But it feels too coherent. Dreams fray at the edges; this has a terrible logic. I count prime numbers to steady myself.

2, 3, 5, 7, 11… Prime 23, my age.

Prime 101, the highway I drove yesterday. Coincidence or pattern?

A flicker. A spark. A distant star? No. Too close. A tiny point of light, expanding, warping into something more.

Numbers….

Equations….

F = ma………E = mc².

They glow in the dark, like ghosts of a forgotten lesson. I reach out. My fingers slip through them, scattering the symbols like smoke…

Why show me this? I whisper, but the void absorbs the words. The equations reform, dancing.

v = v₀ + at

Velocity equals initial velocity plus acceleration multiplied by time. But my v₀ was zero. I wasn’t moving before I fell. So why does it feel like I’ve been falling forever?

The absurdity hits me, sudden and sharp.

Forever..

Maybe that’s it. Maybe this is an orbit. A never-ending free fall around some unseen centre. But orbits curve. I’m moving straight. Aren’t I? Without a reference point, how can I know?

The light fades. Darkness reclaims me. I let my body go limp, a leaf in a hurricane-less wind. And then — it clicks. This isn’t physics. Physics has rules, constants, symmetries. This is something else. The fall isn’t through space, but through mind. The equations are a breadcrumb trail of my own rationality trying to scaffold the abyss.

The fear dissolves. Not because I understand, but because I don’t. There’s a raw beauty in not-knowing. I spread my arms wider, grinning like a madman. Let the void take me. Let the equations fail. Some truths aren’t meant to be solved — only felt.

The truth is, I’ve always been falling. All of us are.

Gravity’s just a habit…
