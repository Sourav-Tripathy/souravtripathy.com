A Sugar Rush of Thoughts

February 16, 2025 / 2 min read Read on Substack ↗

There is a constant battle within me… I don’t know what this battle is about, but I feel it every day. My mind seems to spiral through a world of thoughts …so random and infinite that my heart thumps louder, signaling me to stop, to take a pitstop. Yet, the heart does not force the mind to halt, it approves the journey but gently reminds me to pause.And in that pause, I feel the battle.

It’s not a battle between mind and heart. It’s me vs. me. It sounds nonsensical, I know. But this battle roars within me. I fear that if I slow down, if I listen carefully to that roar to understand it, I might fall… and my heart might stop thumping.

It’s weird. Everything is weird. And yet, the very roar I fear pulls me deeper into my thoughts, making me seek them out. In a way, maybe that is what happiness is. Some might call it a sugar rush, insisting that contentment should be the goal. But who cares what feels “right” in that sense?

The very absurdity of life ensures that the sugar rush never lasts. And that…that’s the beauty of it and I like it…
